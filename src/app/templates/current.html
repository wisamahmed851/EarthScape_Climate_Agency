Current weather versus historical reference

Open-Meteo modelled values compared with a NASA POWER reanalysis climatology (2001-2025, same UTC hour, +-7 calendar days, mean and typical spread +-1 SD). These are two different models on different grids, not two measurements of one thing; differences of a degree or two can be model or grid differences, not weather. Only temperature and relative humidity are comparable; wind (10 m vs 2 m), pressure (hPa vs kPa), precipitation and PM2.5 are not compared.
