Anomaly detection (daily mean temperature)

Method: day-of-year climatology fitted on 2001-2015 only; robust z-score (median/MAD by month) with |z| > 3.5; an Isolation Forest (contamination 1%, fitted on 2001-2015 only) as a second opinion. Both applied to 2016-2025. This is unsupervised: there are no ground-truth labels, so flags are unusual days relative to the training climate, not confirmed events. Because the climate warms in the test years, positive flags may increase.
